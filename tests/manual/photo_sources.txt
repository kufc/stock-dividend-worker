額外標記圖片的來源（embeddings_standard.npz / embeddings_accurate.npz 裡 image_groups 不是空字串的那些）
Sources of the extra labelled photo set used by dump_embeddings.py --extra-dir.
All images come from public, non-gated datasets on the Hugging Face Hub, were re-saved as JPEG
(longer side <= 1024 px) into <extra-dir>/<group>/NNN.jpg, and are NOT committed (only embeddings are).
Images were picked at random across each dataset (fixed random seeds), then every group was checked
by eye on a contact sheet and obviously wrong or ambiguous images were deleted (noted per group).
"License" is what the dataset card / its license tag states; "not stated" = the card has no license.

== Groups that are default categories ==

人像 (44 images)
  - Ryan-sjtu/ffhq512-caption, default/train, one random parquet shard, 25 random rows (FFHQ face portraits,
    every image is one person by construction). License: mit (card tag; FFHQ itself is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). 24 kept
    (1 removed: two faces in frame).
  - detection-datasets/coco, default/val (shard val-00000-of-00002), 25 random images whose annotations contain
    exactly ONE "person" box covering 25-85 % of the image and no other object larger than 30 % of that box.
    License: not stated on the card (COCO annotations are CC BY 4.0, images are Flickr). 20 kept
    (5 removed: only hands/feet visible, a CG avatar, food dominating the frame).

合照 (45 images)
  - CUHK-CSE/wider_face, data/WIDER_val.zip + wider_face_split.zip (validation set). Only the event folders
    "12--Group" and "20--Family_Group", and only images whose annotation has 3-15 faces of at least 40x40 px
    and at most 3 smaller faces; 50 random. License: cc-by-nc-nd-4.0. 45 kept (5 removed: a web-page
    screenshot, a collage, a show poster, two where the people are not the subject).

貓 (50 images) / 狗 (47 images)
  - microsoft/cats_vs_dogs, default/train, labels 0 = cat -> 貓, 1 = dog -> 狗; 50 random rows per label over
    the whole split. License: unknown (card tag). 狗: 3 removed (one mislabelled non-dog image, one where
    the animal is not recognisable, one thumbnail-sized).

    group, 1-2 random images per class:
    風景: badlands, beach, butte, canyon, cliff, coast, creek, desert sand, desert vegetation, forest broadleaf,
      forest needleleaf, hill, islet, lake natural, marsh, mountain, mountain snowy, ocean, river, sky,
      snowfield, valley, waterfall block/fan/plunge, iceberg, field wild, rainforest, sea cliff, volcano, wave,
      sandbar, pond, swamp, rock arch.
    城市建築: abbey, alley, apartment building outdoor, bridge, building facade, castle, cathedral outdoor,
      church outdoor, courthouse, crosswalk, fire station, hotel outdoor, house, inn outdoor, library outdoor,
      lighthouse, mansion, mosque outdoor, motel, office building, pagoda, palace, plaza,
      residential neighborhood, shopfront, skyscraper, street, synagogue outdoor, temple east asia, tower,
      viaduct.
    License: not stated on the card.

美食 (50 images)
  - ethz/food101, default/validation (shard validation-00000-of-00003), 50 random rows; every image in the
    dataset is a dish. License: unknown (card tag).

花草植物 (50 images)
  - nelorth/oxford-flowers, default/test, 30 random rows (Oxford 102 Flowers; all flowers). License: unknown (card tag).
  - bhargob11/houseplants, files under houseplant_images/healthy/, 20 random JPEGs (potted house plants).

交通工具 (50 images)

螢幕截圖 (52 images)

文件收據 (50 images)

== Skipped groups ==
None - all 17 groups were collected.

== Known limits ==
- 人像: half are tight face crops (FFHQ); the COCO half are full-body / action shots of one person.
- 合照: includes candid groups (meetings, people at a table), not only posed group photos.
- 動漫插畫: the anime part is poster-like key visuals with title text; the cartoon part is TV-cartoon frames.
- none_poster: the advertising posters are Chinese e-commerce posters, some show a product or a model.
- A few 風景 images contain a small building or person; a few 城市建築 classes are single houses.
